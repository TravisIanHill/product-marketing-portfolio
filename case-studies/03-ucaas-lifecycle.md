# Cloud Communications: Launch, Onboarding & Retention

**The challenge.** Selling a complex cloud communications (UCaaS) partner offer did not guarantee configuration, activation, adoption or a good support experience. Customers needed a clear answer to "what happens next," and the business needed visibility into recurring post-install friction.

**What I built.**
- Extended product marketing past launch into onboarding measurement, customer communications, support content, adoption and retention
- Developed the onboarding timeline and communications, digital self-service resources and cross-functional feedback mechanisms
- Defined lifecycle KPIs and secured cross-functional agreement on them; program analysis identified incomplete onboarding as the primary driver of churn
- Helped launch a dedicated digital onboarding experience as shared Product and PMM/CX program work

**Impact.** Lifecycle improvements helped **hold churn at or below 3%** while installed seats grew to **more than 70% above forecast**. This was a larger program effort led by Product; my contribution was the lifecycle metrics, communications and self-service workstreams.

**Capabilities:** UCaaS · Lifecycle marketing · Onboarding · Adoption · Retention · Digital self-service · CX
