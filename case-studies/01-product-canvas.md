# Product Marketing Operating System & Product Canvas

**The challenge.** Teams needed a shared strategic source that could bridge product truth, customer needs, positioning, discovery, proof, differentiation and downstream content without recreating the logic for every asset. Traditional product briefs explained the problem and solution, but translation into customer- and segment-relevant language was inconsistent.

**What I built.**
- Co-developed, with a product marketing peer, an expanded internal implementation of the Product Canvas, adapting Pragmatic Institute principles into a reusable PMM source layer
- Authored assigned Product Canvases through review and approval across multiple technology categories
- Connected the framework to segment relevance, reasons to believe, discovery and downstream messaging, so it worked as a strategic input rather than another product brief

**Impact.** The framework became a single source of truth aligning Product, Marketing, Sales, Content and leadership. Completed, approved Canvases across categories show it was adopted in day-to-day work, not just proposed.

**Capabilities:** PMM systems · Positioning · Messaging architecture · Discovery · Segmentation · Knowledge management
