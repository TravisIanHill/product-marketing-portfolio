# AI-Enabled Marketing, Adoption & Governance

**The challenge.** AI experimentation is easy to start and hard to turn into reliable business value without structured source knowledge, reusable instructions, clear roles, human review and governance.

**What I built.**
- Applied Microsoft Copilot, Glean, Claude and n8n to real research, content-development and workflow problems
- Prompt and agent designs built on approved sources, structured inputs, defined output requirements and brand guidance
- Contributions to operating-model and governance materials covering workflow redesign, acceptance criteria, testing, training and go/no-go accountability
- A personal Career Intelligence System that turns distributed career evidence into repeatable analysis and role-specific outputs, with attribution and confidentiality guardrails built in

**Impact.** Practical AI fluency shown through business application, prompt architecture, workflow design, knowledge structuring and human-in-the-loop review. I don't claim unsourced productivity gains.
