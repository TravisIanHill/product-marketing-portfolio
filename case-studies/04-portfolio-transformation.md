# Portfolio Transformation, Migration & Lifecycle

**The challenge.** Merger-era portfolio changes affected far more than product names. Packaging, seller eligibility, customer treatment, grandfathering, moves/adds/changes, replacement offers, migration, support and operational requirements all needed coordinated treatment.

**What I built.**
- Decision-ready lifecycle views combining disposition, dates, owners, replacements, new-sales rules, grandfathering, change treatment, exceptions and source validation
- Structured workstreams that Marketing, Digital, Sales, Operations and support teams could execute from ambiguous and changing decisions
- A clear separation of cosmetic, commercial and lifecycle/operational change, which reduced false equivalence and surfaced hidden dependencies

**Impact.** Clearer control over portfolio transitions, earlier visibility into dependencies, and changing decisions turned into practical readiness and customer-treatment plans. Assigned PMM deliverables moved through execution and approval.

**Capabilities:** Portfolio transformation · Lifecycle management · Migration · Change enablement · Readiness · Risk and dependency management
