# Business Services Operations Build & Scale

**The challenge.** Rapid business voice adoption and multi-state B2B growth required centralized operating capacity instead of startup-style workarounds. Orders from inside and field sales had to move reliably through processing, scheduling, dispatch, installation, confirmation and support.

**What I built.**
- Helped build the people, processes, staffing and capacity plans, CRM workflows, service-delivery handoffs and support infrastructure needed to scale
- Developed budget, staffing and workflow requirements alongside launch and process records
- Connected sales demand to centralized processing, scheduling, dispatch and continued project flow

**Impact.** The operating model supported roughly **75 to 100 projects and installs per day** across a **19-state footprint**. The business reached its subscriber targets ahead of the original rollout timing, grew **31%+ annually**, and the CRM platform supported **125+ users**. These are team and business outcomes; my role was building the operating infrastructure that made the growth repeatable.

**Why it matters for product marketing:** this is where I learned to look past whether something launched and ask whether the business can actually sell, deliver and support it.

**Capabilities:** Business building · Operations leadership · Capacity planning · CRM and workflow · Service delivery · Cross-functional leadership
