# How I Lead and Work

Good systems make sound judgment easier, accountability clearer and improvement more repeatable.

**Don't walk past the problem.** Notice what isn't working, understand it, address what you responsibly can, and route what remains to the right owner.

**Find the pattern, fix the right thing.** Define good, examine the signal, diagnose whether it's people, process, technology or a combination, and avoid treating symptoms as the problem.

**Start with what they need.** Shape detail, hierarchy, pace and format around how the audience will actually use the answer.

**Work backward from done.** Ask the receiving person what complete, correct and usable looks like, then build the inputs, owners and handoffs to get there.

**Learn by putting it to work.** Don't confuse perfection with readiness. Put a sound version into real use, observe honestly and improve from what the work teaches.

**Leave it better than you found it.** Capture enough structure, context and knowledge that the improvement lasts and the next person can succeed without you in the middle.

*Operating loop: notice, diagnose, understand the need, define done, learn through use, leave it better, then listen to the new signal and begin again.*
